# Quiet Queue marketplace contract

- `manifest.json` must use the full 500-character allowance in both description fields and preserve the distinction between Quiet Queue-owned DND and pre-existing native DND.
- `assets/banner.svg` must identify Quiet Queue, its 25/50-minute sessions, and the promise that native notification history remains intact.
- The marketplace preview must be an uncropped 1280x720 frame captured from the isolated real Omarchy shell with a clean plugin log and exact source/package hashes.
- Before capture, the isolated shell must begin with native DND off; the shipped helper must start a 25-minute session; a second scan must prove DND on, valid remaining time, and Quiet Queue ownership.
- The opened panel must visibly show the active owned session, countdown, 25/50-minute actions, end action, and native-history trust boundary.
- A human must inspect the final marketplace-scale image and bind approval to its SHA-256 before submission.
